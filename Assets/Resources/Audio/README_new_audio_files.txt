NEW AUDIO FILES NEEDED
======================
Add the following WAV/MP3/OGG files here (Assets/Resources/Audio/) to unlock full audio:

BACKGROUND MUSIC (looping, one per level):
  BGMusic1.wav  – Level 1 background music
  BGMusic2.wav  – Level 2 background music
  BGMusic3.wav  – Level 3 background music

AMBIENT (looping atmospheric sound):
  Ambient.wav   – Wind, cave hum, or environment ambient

NARRATOR VOICE LINES (one per level):
  NarratorLevel1.wav – "Welcome, brave adventurer! The ancient path of tiles awaits..."
  NarratorLevel2.wav – "You've reached the Bridge of Shadows!..."
  NarratorLevel3.wav – "The final trial — the Crystal Labyrinth!..."
  (Record with text-to-speech or a voice actor; the game falls back gracefully if missing)

SOUND EFFECTS:
  Dance.wav  – Short upbeat jingle/riff played when player dances (F key)
  Fall.wav   – Whoosh / crash for wrong-tile fall sequence

The existing clips (RightJump, WrongJump, LevelComplete, etc.) are already present and working.

NOTE: If you add clips via Inspector on the AudioManager prefab/component,
      you can skip the Resources folder path requirement for those fields.
